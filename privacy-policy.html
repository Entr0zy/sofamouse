SofaMouse Privacy Policy

Updated 25 November 2026

SofaMouse (the phone app) and SofaMouse Helper (the computer app) are made by ENTR0ZY. They collect no personal data.

What stays on your devices

What you type, pointer movements and clipboard text go straight from your phone to your own computer over your local Wi-Fi (or Bluetooth on Android), end-to-end encrypted. Nothing is sent to ENTR0ZY or anyone else, and nothing is stored on a server.

The app keeps its settings and the keys for computers you paired on your phone; the pairing keys sit in the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore. The helper keeps the keys for paired phones in a file in your user folder. "Forget", or deleting the app, removes them.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

There's no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking in either app.

Permissions

Local network: to find and talk to the SofaMouse Helper on your computer. Motion sensors: for the air mouse and back tap, used only on the phone. Bluetooth (Android): to act as a keyboard and mouse for your computer.

Children

The apps collect no data from anyone, including children.
