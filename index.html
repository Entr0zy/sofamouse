SofaMouse

Use your phone as a keyboard, trackpad and air mouse for your computer from across the room. On iPhone it connects over your Wi-Fi to the free SofaMouse Helper, end-to-end encrypted.

1. Get the free helper for your computer

WindowsSofaMouse-Helper-Windows.exe · Windows 10 and 11 · no install needed Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer)SofaMouse-Helper-mac.zip · macOS 11 or newer Mac with IntelSofaMouse-Helper-mac-intel.zip · macOS 11 or newer

Windows: open the file. If Windows SmartScreen says it doesn't recognise the app, choose More info → Run anyway. When Windows Firewall asks, allow SofaMouse Helper on Private networks.

Mac: unzip, move SofaMouse Helper to Applications and open it. If macOS says it can't check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. Then allow SofaMouse Helper under Accessibility so it can move the pointer and type. The helper shows a button that takes you there.

2. Pair your phone (once)

  1. Put the phone and the computer on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. The helper shows a 6-digit PIN.
  3. In SofaMouse, tap the status at the top → Wi-Fi → tap your computer → enter the PIN.
  4. That's it. Next time SofaMouse reconnects by itself with no PIN.

Can't see your computer in the list? Tap Enter the laptop address yourself and type the address the helper shows.

Is it safe?

Yes. The PIN pairing uses SPAKE2, so nobody else on the Wi-Fi can learn the PIN, even by recording the traffic. Every keystroke and movement after that is encrypted with XChaCha20-Poly1305. There's no account and no server, and nothing leaves your network.

Common questions

Does it work with Bluetooth? On Android, SofaMouse can be a real Bluetooth keyboard and mouse with nothing to install. Apple doesn't let iPhone apps do that, so on iPhone it uses Wi-Fi and the helper.

Forgetting a phone or computer. In the helper, tap Forget next to the phone. In the app, go to Settings → Paired computers → Forget.
